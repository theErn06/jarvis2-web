User Guide

System Overview

Welcome to the Jarvis Smart Fridge dashboard. This application synchronizes directly with your local smart refrigerator hardware, allowing you to monitor your groceries and track food waste from anywhere.

Website Navigation Guide

  • 📦

    Live Inventory

    View your current fridge stock in real-time. The table displays exact expiration dates. Expired items display a bold red warning.

  • 🤖

    Remote Jarvis Console

    Visit the Jarvis tab to talk to your fridge from your mobile phone! Simply type or speak your command into the terminal.
